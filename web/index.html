    What this project measured

    Every figure below is generated by the analysis scripts in this repository from real Channel State Information — captured by an ESP32-S3 mesh in the open-source RuView dataset, processed by this project's own pipeline. Nothing is simulated, and where the evidence is negative it is shown as such.

    Design, validated

    1 · Which subcarriers to trust

    Respiration is estimated from the median of the top-K highest-variance subcarriers. Measured against cross-node agreement, a single subcarrier (K=1) and the average of all of them are both spurious — their agreement is no better than a decorrelated control. An intermediate K is genuinely best, which justifies the pipeline's K=8 with numbers rather than assertion.

    Result, refuted by its own test

    2 · Why breathing is not claimed

    Over the full 38-minute recording, the two nodes' respiration estimates correlate at +0.30 against near-zero controls — which looks like a real shared signal. But split the recording into seven-minute segments and re-measure: four of five segments go negative. A breath-by-breath measurement would survive segmentation. This does not, so the long-window agreement reflects slow common structure, not respiration. The result was built, then the test that broke it was built, and both are reported.

    Detection, quantified

    3 · How good is the motion detection?

    The part that works — motion/presence detection — is characterised against a controlled stimulus: real quiet CSI with a motion-like perturbation injected at a known signal-to-noise ratio. The detector reaches AUC 0.94 at SNR 1 and 1.00 by SNR 2, needs motion ≈1.8× the quiet level to fire reliably, and false-alarms on genuine quiet windows only 0.5% of the time. On real data it fires on 36% of the labelled moving-person recording versus 9% of the quiet one. Honest scope: this is a sensitivity characterisation, not field accuracy — that needs a ground-truth sensor.

    Discrimination

    4 · The pipeline discriminates

    On a two-minute "mixed-activity" recording the same pipeline returns cross-node agreement of −0.157 — correctly finding no shared signal when the subject is moving. On the long still-period recording it finds +0.30. A method that reaches opposite conclusions on different data is doing something real, not always saying yes.

    Method, negative

    5 · Phase does not help here

    In theory breathing lives in signal phase. But raw ESP32 phase is corrupted by hardware offsets, and after the standard single-antenna sanitization it is no better than amplitude for respiration on this data. The method that does work — multi-antenna conjugate multiplication — needs contiguous multi-antenna frames this capture does not provide. This identifies a precise hardware requirement for future work.

    Hardware, negative

    6 · Why dedicated hardware is required

    Commodity WiFi cannot do this. The laptop's own adapter (MediaTek MT7922) reports a frozen signal strength — 99 samples over 30 s via netsh, 236 samples over 12 s querying the driver directly, zero variation in both. The driver smooths away exactly the fluctuation sensing depends on. That negative result is what motivates CSI-capable hardware such as the ESP32-S3.
